# Third-party notices and provenance

The MIT license in this repository applies to its original code, documentation, model weights, and generated numerical traces. It does not replace licenses of separately installed dependencies.

The portable model and tooling are adapted from the project's original Python implementation. The released model weights were trained within this project; no pretrained third-party world-model weights are embedded. The trajectories were generated by the project's simulator. The static viewer uses browser APIs and original HTML/CSS/JavaScript, without bundled third-party fonts, scripts, or analytics.

Runtime/build dependencies include PyTorch, NumPy, safetensors, and Python packaging tools. Their license texts are supplied by their distributions and upstream projects. Installing them is separate from the original-code license grant here. A dependency is not made MIT merely because this kit is MIT.

The narrated project video, downloaded speech models, personal notebooks, private conversation exports, local caches, and machine-specific environment are excluded from the release.

Source-record identifiers and SHA-256 hashes in the documentation provide provenance. References to original project-relative paths identify records in the source archive; they are not promises that every original record is present in this focused release.
